Each flash is one real reanalysis column: its lifted-parcel buoyancy curve (left), its location, and its date. The shaded layers show where and when the currently filtered topology lives: a 2°-binned density on the map, a 10-day rose on the ring, and yearly counts on the track. Curve and markers take the color of the profile's topology class. The readout compares CAPE under the legacy topmost-level LNB (E_top) and the max-work LNB (E_max) — it turns red when the legacy rule returns zero for a convectively viable profile. Parcels: A = ambient air lifted as-is; B = boundary-layer air moisture-enriched by the storm's converged central pressure; C = air saturated at the sea-surface temperature. Space bar toggles play. Click any marginal to filter the samples: the map selects a position (±1,500 km), the rings a day-of-year window (±15 days) or an hour-of-day window (±3 h UTC), the track a single year; each marginal keeps showing the density under the other filters, with its own selection dashed. Click the × chips or press Escape to clear. The condition chips restrict everything to profiles where the LNB conventions disagree: T/M/R lanes on the scope mark each convention's LNB (T = legacy topmost, M = max-work, R = lifted-ballistic; the first-crossing convention is omitted — it is materially wrong too often to be interesting); CAPE and potential intensity per convention appear in the readout (PI computed at tcpyPI's own ptop = 50). The eight real columns where the legacy convention fails to converge are included — select "legacy non-convergent" to see them.
